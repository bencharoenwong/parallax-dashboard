How these are counted

Weeks run Monday to Sunday in . "Same point" compares the elapsed part of the current week or month with the same span of the previous one.

Merged: every merged PR in the active repos of the announcements config. Announced: entries in the Shipped section of the vault Feature Timeline on main, by entry date. "In review" entries sit in the auto-filed Candidates block and are not yet curated. Entries in an open review PR count once it merges.

Refreshed every 6 hours by GitHub Actions.
